[ About Foyer ]

Built for the moments buyers have questions.

Foyer is built by ReachLLM Inc., a small product team focused on making AI useful inside real buyer journeys, not just inside chat windows.

We started with brand visibility and answer-engine optimization for teams that needed to understand how AI systems talk about them. Foyer brings that same practical lens to the website itself: answer the visitor, guide the page, capture the lead, and show the operator exactly what happened.

Answer

The agent speaks from the customer's own pages, pricing, FAQs, and uploaded context.

Guide

The page moves with the conversation so visitors can see the section or offer being explained.

Capture

Qualified leads, transcripts, and intent signals land in the dashboard for follow-up.
