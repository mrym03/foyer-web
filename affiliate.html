[ FAQ ]

Common questions.

How does "recurring" actually work?

If a customer you referred pays Foyer $299 every month, you earn $74.75 every month — for as long as they remain a customer. No cap, no expiration. If they stay for 3 years, you get paid for 3 years.

Who is a good fit for this program?

Newsletter writers, YouTubers, agencies, consultants, freelancers, web devs, and anyone whose audience runs a website that could use a voice sales agent — SaaS founders, local services, e-commerce, real estate, recruiting.

You don't need a huge audience. One well-placed mention to the right reader can pay you for years.

When do I get paid?

Monthly via PayPal, once your balance crosses $50. Commissions are held for 30 days to cover refunds, then become payable.

What counts as a referral?

Anyone who clicks your unique link and signs up for a paid Foyer plan within 60 days. We use last-click attribution — if multiple affiliates refer the same person, the last link they clicked wins.

Can I promote on paid ads?

You can run paid ads, but you can't bid on "Foyer", "tryfoyer", or other branded keywords on Google, Bing, or other search engines. Anything else — content, audience targeting, social — is fair game.

Can I refer myself or my own business?

No. Self-referrals are not allowed and will be voided. If you want to use Foyer for your own business, sign up directly — we'd love to have you as a customer.

Do you provide creative assets?

Yes. After you sign up, your Rewardful dashboard includes the logo, screenshots, sample copy, and short-form video clips you can drop into newsletters, tweets, and reviews.

What if a customer cancels and re-subscribes later?

If they come back on the same Stripe account, you continue to earn commissions on their new payments. If they sign up with a totally new account, attribution starts fresh.

Can you change the commission later?

We can change the program for future referrals, but commissions on customers you've already referred stay locked at the rate you signed up under. What you earn on existing referrals is grandfathered.

How do I sign up?

Apply through our Rewardful page. Most approvals come through the same day. Once approved, your dashboard, link, and assets are ready to use immediately.
